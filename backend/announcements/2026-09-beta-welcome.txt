Thanks for being one of the first people on AniLounge. The site is still in beta, and this is our first announcement email, so here's a quick look at what's new and where things are headed.

What's new:
• Voice actor and studio pages: click any cast member or studio to see everything they've worked on, and add them to your favorites.
• A reworked home page with your watchlist activity, new episode releases, and a character of the day.
• Customizable profiles you can share, with tabs for favorites, watchlist, and watch stats.
• Dark mode, email verification, and optional two-factor authentication to keep your account safe.

Coming next: friends, direct messages, and a forum for reviews and discussion.
